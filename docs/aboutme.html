My journey

I am an early-career systems and infrastructure engineer with a Computer Science background. My work sits between systems administration, network engineering, DevOps-style automation, and internal software development.

I like work where the answer is not just "fix the computer." The best projects usually start by understanding an old system, finding the fragile parts, designing a cleaner architecture, automating the repetitive pieces, and moving it into production carefully.

Systems

  • Proxmox virtualization and VM provisioning
  • Active Directory, Samba AD, DNS, LDAP, and Group Policy
  • Shared storage with NAS, NFS, iSCSI, and TrueNAS
  • Endpoint, printer, phone, tablet, and POS support

Software
